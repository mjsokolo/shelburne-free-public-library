First, go to [cwmars.org](http://www.cwmars.org/)

Click on MY ACCOUNT in the top upper right of the page. This will take you to the Login page.

You will need your library card number, which is located just under the barcode on your library card. And you will need your password. If you don’t have your card and password  any CW MARS member library can get your number for you and generate a new password, or you can request a password reset on the login page if you have your library card number. If you plan to use the online catalog you will need to keep a record of your account number and password or the system will remember it for you if you click the “stay logged in?” box.

If you need assistance call the SFPL at 413-625-0307 or email  sfplibrary@cwmars.org

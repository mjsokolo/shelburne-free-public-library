To set up your account so you can see a list of items you have checked out, starting with the date that you set the preference.

1.  Go to:  [cwmars.org](http://www.cwmars.org/)
2.  Go to: **Login to your Library Account**
3.  Login using your library card number and password
4.  Go to: **Account Preferences**
5.  Go to: **Search and History Preferences**
6.  Check the box next to **“keep history of checked out items?”**
7.  Check the box next to **” keep a history of holds?”**
8.  Hit **SAVE**

Then, to view your “Checked Out History”

1.  Go to:  [cwmars.org](http://www.cwmars.org/)
2.  Login as in step 3
3.  Go to: **Items Checked Out**
4.  Go to: **Check Out History**

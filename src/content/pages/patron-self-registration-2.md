[Get a C/W MARS library card](https://shelburne.cwmars.org/cwmars_card)

For a physical library card, visit a C/W MARS member library with a photo ID and proof of address. Eligible Massachusetts residents aged 13 or older who do not already have a C/W MARS library card can apply online for an eCard to access electronic materials. See the link above for full eligibility requirements.

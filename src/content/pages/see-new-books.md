##   See new titles added to the SFPL catalog in the last 14 days.

To see new titles added to the SFPL collection in the last 14 days, go to [CW MARS](http://www.cwmars.org/) and click on the EXPLORE  drop down menu and click on NEW TITLES.

Once on the new titles page, scroll down and look for **Shelburne Free Public Library.** ( Middle column near the bottom). Click on it and you will be taken to a page in the catalog that displays our new titles. From here you can place a hold and read reviews.
